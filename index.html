Bechtel-EnkaPayments2020 – 2022

Thousands of payment orders, no more typing

01

The routine. I processed vendor and staff payments for a large motorway construction project.

02

The problem. The company's in-house ERP had no link to the local e-banking platform (Halcom), so every order was typed in by hand: permits and fees for about 2,000 expat workers (renewed every three months, three orders each), their monthly salaries, and around 100 vendor payments a week.

03

What I did. Built an Excel workbook that generates the bank's import file (TXT) straight from the payment data, so a whole batch loads into Halcom in one step. I also wrote a small Python script that filled in payment slips on the government ePlati portal and saved the payment reference numbers back to the sheet.

ResultWork that needed two people was done by me alone, with far fewer entry errors. The workbook is still in use.

TeletraderMarket data2015 – 2020

Spin-off adjustments ready on day one

01

The routine. Most of my day was reviewing reports from error-detection checks on market data (outliers, outages, missing prices) for stocks, bonds and funds from many sources.

02

The problem. When a company spins off part of its business, its price history has to be adjusted, or the chart shows a crash that never happened. The adjustment factor was copied from competitor platforms, so we had to wait for it, and it was not available for every market we covered.

03

What I did. Worked out a method that combines the public spin-off terms with pre-trading data available to us internally, so the factor can be calculated before trading starts.

ResultAdjusted prices on the spin-off date itself, at least a day earlier than before, for every market we covered. The method is still in use.

Orange TrailAR · APRemote · 2022 – 2025

Billing data that pulls itself in

01

The routine. Day-to-day accounting for several group companies: invoicing, vendor payments, reconciliations and payroll.

02

The problem. Clients were billed on their Google Ads spend, and a part-time colleague copied those figures by hand from each account's dashboard. On the AP side, daily invoices from Meta in USD and EUR took at least an hour a day to check.

03

What I did. Built Google Sheets scripts that pull the spend directly from Google Ads, replacing the manual copying. For the Meta invoices I built a Power Query model that aggregates the spend data for the check.

ResultBilling figures arrive automatically and accurately instead of being retyped, and the daily invoice check starts from one table instead of scattered exports.

Bechtel-EnkaTax2020 – 2022

A fuel excise refund, set up from zero

01

The routine. Alongside AP, payments and VAT records, I handled correspondence with the Tax Administration.

02

The problem. The company was entitled to a refund of excise duty on transport fuel (REF-T), but had no procedure for claiming it.

03

What I did. Took part from the very first meeting with the tax inspector to the final claim: agreed with the Tax Administration how data and documents would be submitted, collected the documentation and filed the refund claims.

ResultA working procedure, agreed with the Tax Administration, for regular refund claims. The hard part was not filing a claim but getting the whole process defined and accepted.
